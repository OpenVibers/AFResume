Every video, clip, thumbnail, paste and avatar on the network lives here, keyed by tenant app. Apps authenticate with constant-time keys or offline-verified user tokens, and cross-tenant access is a hard 403.

**Recording.** VODs are pulled from RTMP or ingested over RTP/SDP with codec passthrough, then finalized through remux, probe, master-recovery, thumbnail and webhook steps. A background health scanner probes, decodes and quarantines broken files.

**Storage tiering.** Files move from local disk to Backblaze B2 and Cloudflare R2 under disk pressure and play back through presigned URLs. Two production incidents shaped it: ghost rows starving the drain, and a file-descriptor leak from aborted range requests pinning 81 GB of unlinked files. Both are now guarded by design, including an fd watchdog.

**Clips.** Failed cuts retry with backoff and pull cloud VODs back to hot storage on the second attempt, because seeking a fragmented MP4 over HTTP scans the whole file.

**Privacy.** View counting is per visitor per six hours using an HMAC of the IP; raw addresses are never stored. Avatars from any site are ingested server-side, SSRF-checked and re-encoded, so no client-supplied image URL is ever stored or served.
