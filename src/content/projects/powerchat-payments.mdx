One payment-first ledger and one idempotent `confirmCharge`, fed by three racing triggers (provider webhook, the tipper's return poll and a nightly sweep), behind a registry of providers: Stripe Connect Express, Square, PayPal partner referrals, PowerChat Pay on an NMI tokenized vault, and NowPayments crypto invoices. The tipper picks the method explicitly; an unavailable pick is a 400, never a silent fallback onto a different processor.

**Ledgers.** Streamer and crypto balances are append-only with a schema-unique external identity per entry, so double-crediting is structurally impossible. Payout submission is gated on an independent sum recompute; mismatches park as held with a reason, and failures reverse through explicit entries rather than deletes.

**Recurring billing.** Pro plans and viewer-to-streamer subscriptions charge the vault on our schedule with a 1, 3 and 7-day retry ladder and drift-free period math; entitlements change only from confirmed billing state or an audited admin correction.

**Verification.** Each provider webhook sits behind its own verifier (HMAC-SHA256, PayPal's verify-signature API, HMAC-SHA512 over key-sorted JSON) and a delivery dedupe table. Card data lives in provider-hosted iframes and never touches PowerChat. Validated by a governed integration suite that drives all six rails through the production HTTP composition, plus an empty-PostgreSQL migration proof in CI.
