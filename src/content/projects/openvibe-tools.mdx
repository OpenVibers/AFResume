A gateway plus seven satellite apps serving 169 tools from one catalog: developer utilities, network lookups, image and audio converters, PDF and document tools, text and logo generators, a YouTube downloader, a North America survival map and a food-bank finder.

**One catalog, many addresses.** A host registry gives every tool a canonical host, a short memorable host and 301 aliases, merged every minute with owner overrides from the Network's domain admin. Purchased domains attach to a tool from the admin UI; people are redirected to the short address while crawlers keep the descriptive one. Unknown subdomains redirect to the index so wildcard DNS never renders an invented brand.

**Built for search.** Every tool is server-rendered with no required JavaScript, carries its own title, description, FAQ structured data and sitemap entry, and the index and `llms.txt` are generated from the catalog.

**Operating notes.** YouTube blocks the datacenter address on every player client; the app probes every 30 minutes and shows an honest banner rather than a broken download button.
