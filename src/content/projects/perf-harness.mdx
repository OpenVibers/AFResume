Asked for provable before-and-after numbers rather than assertions, I built the measurement first. `measure-page.js` reports median TTFB, FCP, LCP, CLS, long-task time, bytes and request counts under desktop and throttled-phone profiles; `ab.js` interleaves two variants so machine noise cancels; `trace-page.js` attributes forced layout to the script that caused it; `check-budgets.js` runs in `npm test`.

Precise coverage showed 14 percent of shipped JavaScript executing on the home page, so heavy routes moved behind an inline loader with idle prefetch. Layout-shift attribution found one hero stat board responsible for 72 percent of CLS. Idle-page animation gating cut running animations from 72 to 27 and idle main-thread time by about 41 percent.
